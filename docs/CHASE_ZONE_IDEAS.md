# Continuum Chase zone ideas

The Flooded Maglev Terminal follows Kevin’s transit-tunnel scene. Trains pass on the tracks flanking the rideable spillway. Their wakes sweep across it after a directional warning.

## In reserve

1. **Server Cathedral** — Towering server aisles with timed bursts of coolant.
2. **Midnight Market** — Delivery carts, falling shutters, and stalls that pack themselves up.
3. **Drone Foundry** — Assembly arms swing over the route while new drones join the chase.
4. **Automated Freight Docks** — Cargo shadows show which lane a crane will block.
5. **Glass Skyway** — Gaps in an elevated road expose the city far below.
6. **Memory Suburb** — The same birthday party repeats while the street layout changes.
7. **Rejected Theme Park** — Runaway ride cars and malfunctioning animatronics.
8. **Blackout District** — Emergency lights, code rain, and sound reveal the route.
9. **Moderation Border** — Redaction shutters close lanes during transit clearance.

## New candidates after the terminal

1. **Pneumatic Mail Exchange** — Transparent tubes overhead launch misrouted parcels onto the road. The tube lights signal which lane gets the delivery.
2. **Tidal Pumpworks** — Pump strokes send visible pulses through pipes before pressure gates open and shut.
3. **Weather Factory** — Test bays switch between crosswinds, sheets of rain, and patches of artificial snow.
4. **Kinetic Art Museum** — Moving sculptures sweep across the route, with their projected shadows showing the timing.
5. **Rooftop Laundry Maze** — Climb out from underground onto the roofs and jump between service decks as giant sheets snap across the route.
6. **Drive-In Graveyard** — Old cinema projectors illuminate abandoned cars and expose the gaps between them.
7. **Recycling Rapids** — Tilted sorting belts drop discarded neon props into the street in clearly signaled waves.
8. **Unrendered District** — Wireframes show where barriers will form before the street finishes loading.
9. **Jazz Power Station** — Pistons and swing gates move to a live rhythm that doubles as the warning.
10. **Lost-and-Found Warehouse** — Magnetic racks sweep huge umbrellas, shopping carts, and hundreds of identical left shoes across the lanes.
